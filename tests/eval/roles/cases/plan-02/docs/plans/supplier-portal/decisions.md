# Decisions: supplier-portal

## Round 1

### Q1. Who uses the portal?
1. Supplier staff, with one admin per company; procurement and finance work in a model-driven app (recommended)
2. Procurement only, on behalf of suppliers
3. In your own words
Answer: Supplier staff, with one admin per company; procurement and finance work in a model-driven app

### Q2. How are bank details handled?
1. The supplier admin enters them and the portal never shows them back; only finance reads them (recommended)
2. Procurement enters them
3. In your own words
Answer: The supplier admin enters them and the portal never shows them back; only finance reads them

### Q3. What happens automatically?
1. An approval for procurement, a mail to finance without the details, and a weekly certificate expiry reminder (recommended)
2. An approval only
3. In your own words
Answer: An approval for procurement, a mail to finance without the details, and a weekly certificate expiry reminder

## Round 2

### Q4. Which environments does the solution move through?
1. Dev, Test and Prod, managed in Test and Prod, through the pipeline (recommended)
2. Dev and Prod only
3. In your own words
Answer: Dev, Test and Prod, managed in Test and Prod, through the pipeline

### Q5. Licensing?
1. Power Pages authenticated-user capacity for 3,000 users and per-user plans for the 12 internal users, and a Power Automate Premium licence for the flows' service account (recommended)
2. Anonymous capacity only
3. In your own words
Answer: Power Pages authenticated-user capacity for 3,000 users and per-user plans for the 12 internal users, and a Power Automate Premium licence for the flows' service account

### Q6. Which security rules apply?
1. The company's information security standard and the tenant data policy as it stands (recommended)
2. Only the tenant defaults
3. In your own words
Answer: The company's information security standard and the tenant data policy as it stands
