# Decisions: site-inspections

## Round 1

### Q1. Who uses the app, and from which devices?
1. Inspectors on tablets, planners and one manager on desktops (recommended)
2. Inspectors on phones
3. In your own words
Answer: Inspectors on tablets, planners and one manager on desktops

### Q2. How are inspections and findings stored?
1. Dataverse tables for sites, inspections and findings, with the photo in a file column (recommended)
2. A SharePoint list per table
3. In your own words
Answer: Dataverse tables for sites, inspections and findings, with the photo in a file column

### Q3. How does an urgent finding reach the planners?
1. A Teams post from a flow when the finding is created (recommended)
2. An email to each planner
3. In your own words
Answer: A Teams post from a flow when the finding is created, and a weekday summary by email for findings left open
