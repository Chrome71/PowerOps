# Decisions: starter-onboarding

## Round 1

### Q1. Who works in the app?
1. HR coordinators, line managers, task owners and payroll officers, each with their own view (recommended)
2. HR coordinators only, with managers told by email
3. In your own words
Answer: HR coordinators, line managers, task owners and payroll officers, each with their own view

### Q2. Which personal data is stored?
1. Name, start date, manager and job title, plus bank account and national ID numbers for payroll (recommended)
2. Names and dates only; payroll keeps the rest in its own system
3. In your own words
Answer: Name, start date, manager and job title, plus bank account and national ID numbers for payroll

### Q3. What should happen automatically?
1. Create tasks from a template, remind owners, and tell the manager when all tasks are done (recommended)
2. Create tasks only
3. In your own words
Answer: Create tasks from a template, remind owners, and tell the manager when all tasks are done

## Round 2

### Q4. Which environments does the solution use?
1. Dev and Prod, managed in Prod (recommended)
2. Dev, Test and Prod
3. In your own words
Answer: Dev and Prod, managed in Prod. HR tests in Dev with the coordinators.

### Q5. Licensing and cost?
1. Per-app plans for managers and task owners, per-user for HR and payroll, paid by HR (recommended)
2. Per-user plans for everyone
3. In your own words
Answer: Per-app plans for managers and task owners, per-user for HR and payroll, paid by HR

### Q6. Security and the data policy?
1. The company's HR data protection standard and the tenant data policy as it stands, with roles per user group (recommended)
2. Only the tenant defaults
3. In your own words
Answer: The company's HR data protection standard and the tenant data policy as it stands, with roles per user group
