What happens after you report

Most repairs are looked at on the day you report them. We text you at every step, so you can follow the repair here instead of phoning.

Our repair target times
  1. 1We confirm straight awayYou get a reference by text and email.
  2. 2We look at it within 1 working dayEmergencies are dealt with at once.
  3. 3You choose a visit timeMorning, afternoon or school-run slots.
  4. 4We do the repairEmergency within 6 hours, urgent within 7 days, routine within 28 days.
