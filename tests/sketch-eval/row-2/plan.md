# Repairs desk

## Goal

1. The repairs desk sees which reported repairs need triage first, and books a visit before the target time runs out.
2. The desk logs a repair a tenant reports by phone.

## Screens

A canvas app for the repairs desk, used at a desk and on a phone when on call.

- Repairs (desktop 1366 by 768): who: the repairs coordinator, all day. Job: triage the queue and assign a contractor to the most urgent repair. First look: the time left on the selected emergency.
- Repairs (phone): who: the coordinator on call. Job: triage and assign from anywhere. First look: the top emergency, then the docked action.
- Repair (phone): who: the coordinator on call. Job: read one repair and assign a contractor. First look: the time left before the attend-by time.
- Log a repair (desktop and phone): who: the coordinator, when a tenant phones. Job: record the problem, the home and who is at risk, and save. First look: the first field of the form.

## Assumptions

- The sketch draws these parts its map marks not confirmed, by choice: a smaller grey count in each tab label, the grey fill of the time-used bars after the first, and a close icon on the success banner. Their fallbacks are in the map.
