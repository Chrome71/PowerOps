# Repairs performance

## Goal

1. The head of repairs and the operations lead see each month whether repairs are completed on time against the 90% target, and which trade and contractor cause a miss.

## Screens

A Power BI report, one page, with a phone layout.

- Overview (report page, 1920 by 1080): who: the head of repairs and the operations lead, monthly and before the board. Job: see whether repairs were completed on time last month, and why not. First look: the share completed on time against the 90% target.
- Overview (phone layout): who: the same people, away from a desk. Job: the same answer on a phone. First look: the same number.

## Assumptions

- The phone layout is drawn 390 px wide, a width Learn doesn't state, and the loading state shows the product's own behaviour, whose look was not checked. Both are recorded here by choice, as the map says.
